The Specialist: one device, four classroom journeys

As of 12 November 2026. Everything runs on the Jetson Orin Nano with the network off. Each journey takes its own route through the modes in the middle, and all four share the same textbook core: search, gate, paraphrase, check. The small language model, Qwen2.5-1.5B, does only the three steps marked Qwen: paraphrasing DIKSHA passages, writing an example and rewriting a follow-up. Every other step is DIKSHA text, a specialised model or a rule.

Tap to talk

Tap the 2.8 inch touch screen to start and again to stop. Enter on a USB keyboard also works. Recording stops itself after 30 s

safe request: which mode is it?

J1 Teacher prep: lesson plan

"Yesterday we covered nutrition in plants", then "Lesson plan on photosynthesis". Recap, key ideas, a simpler version, a check question and a stretch question, for a mixed class or one level

J2 Multi-grade revision

"Revise acids with class 7", then "now do the same for class 8". That class's own books first, an explanation with an example, then its textbook questions

J3 Student group quiz

"Quiz us on photosynthesis". A textbook question, a spoken answer, then "That's right" or "Not quite" with the checked answer. Next question until "stop"

J4 Doubts in Hindi or English

"प्रकाश संश्लेषण क्या है?", then "and why sunlight?", answered like any question

Qwen

Memory, in RAM only
The last 3 turns, what the class covered, the quiz in progress and the class being revised. Cleared after 3 minutes of silence, on "new topic" or "that's all", after any safety flag, and at reboot. Nothing about students is stored.
Language
Nobody sets it. The answer comes back in the language of the question, unless the question names one ("in English", "हिंदी में").
At boot
Camera and USB set up, mic, speaker and screen found, Ollama, Bhashini and Qwen loaded, then the screen shows "Tap to ask".
Status, 12 November
All four journeys work on the Mac in English. Hindi runs and real recordings wait for the Jetson. Students use it with a teacher present until a safeguarding review.
